# Suno prompts for three background tracks

Modern glitch / artcore synth, instrumental, made to loop under a dungeon crawler. In Suno use
**Custom mode**, switch **Instrumental** on, paste the *Style* into "Style of Music" and the *Structure* into
"Lyrics" (tags in square brackets, no words). Put the *Exclude* text in "Exclude styles" if your version has it.
Suno follows tempo only loosely, so say the BPM in the style text and pick the take that is closest.
Make 2 to 4 takes of each, keep the best, and name the files as in `README.md`.

All three are kept moderate on purpose: they will play for hours, so they should sit behind the game and
not wear on you. Only the boss is allowed to push.

---

## 1. Dungeon: "Low Orbit" (the main track)

- **File:** `dungeon.ogg` (and `dungeon_2.ogg` for another take)
- **Length:** about 3:00
- **BPM:** 108, key D minor
- **Feel:** curious and focused, a slow pulse you can walk to

**Style (paste):**
`instrumental glitch IDM, modern artcore synth, 108 bpm, D minor, warm analog pads, soft sub kick, crisp ticking hi-hats, granular stutter edits, bitcrushed arpeggios, gentle sidechain, deep reverb, hypnotic, spacious, clean mix`

**Exclude:** `vocals, lyrics, rap, guitar, orchestra, harsh distortion, dubstep drops, loud`

**Structure (paste in Lyrics):**
```
[Instrumental]
[Intro: soft pad, a single ticking glitch pattern, no drums]
[Verse: soft kick enters, plucked synth arpeggio, light stutter edits]
[Build: filter opens slowly, extra percussion, no drop]
[Main groove: warm bass, glitch fills every 4 bars, stays calm]
[Break: pads only, reversed textures]
[Main groove: same as before, slightly fuller]
[Outro: drums fall away, pad fades, ends on a held chord]
```

## 2. Cavern: "Glass Hollow"

- **File:** `cavern.ogg`
- **Length:** about 3:30
- **BPM:** 84, key A minor
- **Feel:** cold, wet and echoing, mostly texture and space

**Style (paste):**
`instrumental ambient glitch, modern artcore, 84 bpm, A minor, crystalline bell arpeggios, dripping bitcrushed clicks, long cavernous reverb, slow evolving pads, sparse soft percussion, granular textures, dark and calm, lots of space`

**Exclude:** `vocals, lyrics, heavy drums, fast tempo, harsh distortion, bright EDM leads, guitar`

**Structure (paste in Lyrics):**
```
[Instrumental]
[Intro: dripping clicks and one low drone, no beat]
[Section A: slow bell arpeggio over a pad, very light kick on beat one]
[Section B: glitchy granular swells, reversed echoes, still sparse]
[Section A: arpeggio returns, a second quiet layer]
[Outro: everything thins out to the drone and the drips]
```

## 3. Troll boss: "Overclock"

- **File:** `boss.ogg`
- **Length:** about 2:30
- **BPM:** 148, key E minor
- **Feel:** driving and tense but still musical. It should feel like a fight, not a panic attack.

**Style (paste):**
`instrumental artcore, modern glitch synth, 148 bpm, E minor, chopped breakbeat drums, gritty saw bass with filter wobble, stuttering glitch edits, detuned supersaw stabs, fast bitcrushed arpeggios, tight sidechain, driving, hypnotic, controlled, clean mix`

**Exclude:** `vocals, lyrics, screaming, metal guitar, harsh noise, distorted clipping, dubstep drops, orchestra`

**Structure (paste in Lyrics):**
```
[Instrumental]
[Intro: filtered breakbeat and a low pulse, 4 bars]
[Verse: gritty bass riff, glitch stutters on the off beats]
[Build: arpeggio climbs, snare roll, no drop]
[Main: full drums, supersaw stabs, steady and driving]
[Break: drums drop to a heartbeat kick, pad]
[Main: same as before, with a counter-melody]
[Outro: drums cut, last stab rings out]
```

## Tips

- **Loops:** pick takes that start and end on a clean bar. If a take ends abruptly, use Suno's "Extend" from the
  last bar, or cut it at a bar line in any audio editor and add a short fade (about 1 second) in and out.
- **Loudness:** export around -14 LUFS (about -3 dB peak) so the three feel the same and the options volume works.
- **Stress check:** if a take feels too busy, rerun it with the words "sparse", "slower" and "calm" at the start
  of the style, or lower the BPM by 10.
